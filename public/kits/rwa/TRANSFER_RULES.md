# Transfer rules worksheet

Who may hold a token with restrictions, the check behind it, and the powers the issuer keeps, written before any token is issued.

| Decision | What to write | Value |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| Jurisdictions | Where holders may be and where they may not. | |\n| Investor class | Retail, accredited, institutional, and the proof of each. | |\n| Identity check | The registry or the attestation that proves eligibility and who issues it. | |\n| List maintainer | Who adds and removes wallets and with what delay. | |\n| Freeze | When a wallet may be frozen, by whom and how it is lifted. | |\n| Forced transfer | When the issuer may move a balance and the record it leaves. | |\n| Recall | When the issuer may recall tokens and what the holder receives. | |\n| Exit rights | The redemption or sale a holder keeps no matter what, and the test that proves it. | |\n| Disclosure | The screen that shows these rules before a purchase. | |

Fill the Value column before the first contract is written. A row left
blank is undecided and should say what would decide it.
