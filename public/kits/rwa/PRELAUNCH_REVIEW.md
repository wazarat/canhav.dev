# RWA pre-launch review

The review passes the CanHav studio records verdicts for, in the order a
reviewer takes them. A verdict is a claim with evidence behind it, so each
pass names what the evidence is. The two passes every shape shares, static
analysis and key custody, apply here too.

1. **Transfer rules match the written policy and exit rights survive them.** Evidence is the transfer rules worksheet beside the tests for allowlist, freeze and forced transfer, and the exit test.
2. **Supply never exceeds the attested reserve.** Evidence is the mint gate test with a stale attestation and the published attestation page.
3. **The waterfall pays and absorbs losses in the written order.** Evidence is the Foundry trace of a default with a partial recovery flowing through the tranches.
4. **Issuer powers are named, keyed and disclosed.** Evidence is the role model with a key and a delay per power, and the holder screen that shows them.

Record each verdict in the studio with a pointer to where the evidence lives
in the repository, or through `update_project` with the pass id.
