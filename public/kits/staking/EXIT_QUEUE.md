# Exit queue worksheet

How a holder of a staking or restaking token gets out, written before the token is listed anywhere.

| Decision | What to write | Value |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| Trigger | What a holder does to start an exit and what they hold while waiting. | |\n| Duration | The time an exit takes in the normal case and the worst case, and what sets it. | |\n| Order | How the queue is served and whether anyone may skip it. | |\n| Display | The place and the date a holder sees, and where. | |\n| Partial exits | Whether a holder can exit part of a position and how it is accounted. | |\n| Liquidity buffer | Any buffer that pays exits early, its size and who funds it. | |\n| Service exits | For a restaking token, the exit of each service and how the longest one bounds the queue. | |\n| Emergency | What happens to the queue when the token is paused. | |

Fill the Value column before the first contract is written. A row left
blank is undecided and should say what would decide it.
